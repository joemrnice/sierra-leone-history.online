About the Digital Archive

Editorial standards, research methodology, and non-partisan historical principles

Mission & Scope

The Sierra Leone Historical Knowledge Center is a zero-framework, research-first digital historical data portal. It serves students, educators, genealogists, researchers, and global citizens by providing verified, cross-linked historical data on Sierra Leone from prehistory through contemporary times.

Editorial Principles

1. Evidence Over Conjecture: All entries prioritize primary archival records, Truth & Reconciliation Commission materials, and peer-reviewed academic literature.
2. Transparent Disagreement: Where historical accounts differ regarding dates or interpretations, competing perspectives are presented transparently.
3. Zero Framework Static Architecture: Built using clean HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript without heavy frontend frameworks or proprietary backend databases.
